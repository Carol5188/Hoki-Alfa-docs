Relationship Explanation

Some friendships deserve a place of their own. Build a bond, grow closer, and celebrate every moment together.

01Choose Your Bond

Brother

Side by side, no explanations needed. Open the mic and share the everyday.

Sister

A safe place for every joy and every little feeling. Always there to listen.

Close Friend

You may not talk every day, but you always understand. Give your bond a name.

◇ All bond types are open to everyone. Choose the one that feels right.

02How to Build a Bond

Meet the requirements below, then send an invitation to someone who matters.

Mutual Friends

Choose someone you trust

500+

Required Bond Points

Open Slots

Available for both people

  1. 1

    Start from a profile

    Open [CP & Friends], tap an empty slot, then choose Brother, Sister, or Close Friend.

  2. 2

    Choose a friend and invite

    Choose an eligible friend with a free slot. Existing bonds and duplicate requests aren't allowed.

  3. 3

    Wait for acceptance

    Once they accept within 24 hours, your bond will appear on both profiles.

3 free slots, unlock up to 5

Brother, Sister, and Close Friend share these slots. CP uses a separate slot. Slots 4 and 5 unlock in order for 99,999 Coins each. Purchased capacity stays with you.

Invitations cannot be withdrawn. While pending, one slot is reserved for each person. Declined or expired invitations release both slots.

03Grow Your Bond

Send gifts or complete bond missions to earn more Bond Points together.

Send something meaningful

After your bond is created, send eligible gifts such as the Together Gift Box to earn Bond Points.

NEW

Lucky gifts do not add Bond Points.

Complete bond missions

Finish daily and weekly missions, and let every shared moment move your bond forward.

Go to Bond Profile → Level Up to see gifts and missions.

04Exclusive Mic Seat Icons

When a bonded friend is on mic, their seat number changes to your bond icon. Everyone else keeps the standard seat number.
